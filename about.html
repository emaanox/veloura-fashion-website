L'Art du Revers · Main Levée

Genèse et Vocation

Rejecting the ephemeral fever of trends.

Founded in Paris under the vaulted skylights of a historic 19th-century workshop, Velour'a arose from a quiet defiance. While contemporary luxury accelerated into disposable cadence, our salon anchored itself in the solemn discipline of permanence. We view garments not as seasonal novelties, but as enduring monolithic architectures sculpted for the human body.

Every garment in our repertory begins as an unhurried architectural dialogue: how does pure silk double-georgette respond to gravity? How does hand-steamed cashmere embrace the shoulders in motion without imposing constriction?

“A garment should feel like an armor of quiet confidence.”

— Madeleine De Vance, Fondatrice & Directrice Artistique EST. 2018
