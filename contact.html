Made-to-measure consultations begin with a 90-minute silhouette profiling in our Paris, Milan, or New York salons. Our master artisans take up to 34 individual anatomical measurements before drafting a custom muslin toile. Two subsequent fittings ensure absolute perfection in drape and comfort.

Lead time: 6 to 10 weeks · Download Fitting Dossier

All haute couture and ready-to-wear works are packaged in temperature-regulated, custom cedar wardrobe boxes with archival garment bags. Orders exceeding $500 enjoy complimentary climate-controlled private courier dispatch worldwide with real-time personal liaison check-ins.

Fully insured transit · Signature verification

Velour'a garments are lifetime investments. We maintain an archive of all past seasonal fabrics, silks, and custom dyed threads. Our preservation atelier accepts past works for restorative cleansing, re-lining, and delicate hand-stitching repairs upon request.
